Account identifiers and profile information are stored with your V2Charge account. The retention and account-closure section explains what is retained and how deletion requests are reviewed. Data we process

  • Account and profile: email address, account identifiers and authentication information used to sign in and manage your account. Profile information you choose to add can include your name, address, country, city and profile photo.
  • Vehicles: vehicle registration, make, model and year that you choose to save.
  • Charging and transactions: charging sessions, orders, energy use, receipts, membership, wallet balances, payments, payment references, vouchers and rewards records needed to operate these features. V2Charge wallet balances can only be used for charging on our platform; they cannot be transferred or withdrawn.
  • Location and search: when you choose to use your location and grant permission, the app uses location to display nearby charging stations and centre the map. Map search can send your search text and location proximity to Mapbox. You can decline location permission and search manually.
  • Diagnostic reports: when you explicitly choose to upload a diagnostic report, we receive structured app navigation, operation and error information, build/platform details, installation and session identifiers, and relevant order references. This helps investigate problems. Sharing requires confirmation for each upload. The diagnostic filters are designed to exclude passwords, verification codes, tokens, card numbers, CVCs and payment keys.
  • Feedback: the feedback text you choose to submit is stored to handle your request.
  • SDK technical data: the app includes Google ML Kit barcode scanning. Google documents collection of app/device information, installation identifiers, performance and error/API-event information for SDK diagnostics and usage analytics. This is separate from a diagnostic report you choose to upload in V2Charge.

V2Charge is intended for adults. It has no advertising or health features. Payment and mapping providers may process technical information for their own service operation; having no advertising features does not mean that no identifiers are processed.

Service providers

We use Logto for authentication, Stripe for payment processing and Mapbox for maps and location search. Google ML Kit supports barcode scanning. Information needed for these features is transmitted to those providers. Stripe's SDK handles payment details; our system stores customer and payment references and transaction records. Mapbox receives map/search requests and can process location and usage telemetry under its policies.

Provider information: Stripe privacy, Mapbox privacy, Mapbox telemetry.

How we protect information

Communication between the app and the public V2Charge service uses HTTPS. Account access requires an authenticated session, and operational access to account and transaction records is controlled by administrator roles. Payment credentials are handled by Stripe; service secrets are held on the server and are not included in public web pages. These controls support the account, charging and payment functions described in this notice.

Retention and account closure

Closing your account is not the same as deleting its data.

The current account closure function disables sign-in and revokes credentials after charging, payments, wallet balances and membership requirements are settled. It does not erase profile, vehicle, transaction or other associated records.

Our system stores account, vehicle and transaction records and does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period to those records. We do not claim that uninstalling the app or closing an account erases them.

Uploaded diagnostic reports are retained indefinitely by default and can be viewed by authorised V2Power SUPER_ADMIN administrators, including future administrators with that role. Where diagnostic sharing is enabled, the report deletion API accepts an authenticated active account request for an individual report. That action removes the report's diagnostic content but retains its associated identifiers, digest, consent and timing records. It does not remove business records, access audit records, backups, local copies or copies already delivered. Closing the account prevents access to that authenticated API.

You can request deletion of your V2Charge account and associated data through info@v2power.io without reinstalling the app. The account owner reviews requests manually, verifies identity and sends a result explaining completed deletion, outstanding actions and any justified retention. Verified whole-account deletion requests are an exception to the diagnostic retention default: identifiable account diagnostics are included in the manual deletion review.

After verified account deletion, we retain only the minimum records necessary for a specific legal obligation or an unresolved dispute. We delete those retained records when the relevant obligation or dispute-related requirement ends; we do not retain them permanently by default. Your case result identifies each retained category, the reason, and any applicable fixed retention period or end condition. Relevant categories can include charging receipts and ledger, wallet settlement, refunds and disputes, memberships, monetary rewards and payment references. Each retained category requires its own specific legal obligation or unresolved dispute; we do not assign a blanket retention period or claim that account closure deletes records. Backup copies and provider-held data are included in the review; we distinguish outstanding work from completed deletion.

Contact V2Charge support

Email info@v2power.io and identify the product as V2Charge. Include your account email and a brief description of your request. Do not send your password, verification codes, full card details or payment credentials.

You may ask about your data, location permissions, uploaded diagnostics, or the difference between account closure and data deletion. If you cannot access your account email, say so in your request.
