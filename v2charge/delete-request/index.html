Account and data deletion request

This page explains how to ask us to delete your V2Charge account and data. V2Charge is our electric vehicle charging app. Requests are handled manually by our team.

Reviewed by the V2Power product owner

How to request · What to include · What happens next · What may be retained · Closure vs deletion

How to request deletion

This is not a web form. Sending a request does not submit, delete or change anything automatically. There is no account sign-in, app reinstall, upload or payment required.

Email us at:

info@v2power.io

Subject line to use:

V2Charge account and data deletion request

You can also copy the address manually if the link above does not open your mail app:

info@v2power.io

The product owner has agreed to review requests sent to this address personally.

What to include

Include only these two things:

  • The email address registered to your V2Charge account.
  • The name “V2Charge”, so we can route your request correctly.

You may add a short note, for example that you want your account and data deleted, or that you cannot access your account email.

Never send your password, one-time or verification codes (OTP), card numbers, CVCs or other payment credentials. We will never ask for these to process a deletion request.

What happens next

  • We read your request and reply to confirm we are handling it.
  • We verify that the request is genuine. This is a case-specific email reply challenge, not a login code or an app OTP. The exact check depends on your case.
  • If you cannot access the registered account email, tell us. We will agree a proportionate recovery step for your case rather than denying the request outright.
  • We check the relevant charging, payment, wallet balance and membership records, and explain any step you need to take first.
  • After verification, we delete the account, profile, saved vehicles, identifiable diagnostic records, and authentication associations for V2Charge.
  • We ask service providers to delete data they hold for us where deletion applies.
  • We send you a completion, pending or retention receipt that reflects what actually happened.

We review requests and work to complete them promptly. We explain any necessary steps or delays in our reply; sending a request does not instantly erase data.

What may be retained

After verified account deletion, we retain only the minimum records necessary for a specific legal obligation or an unresolved dispute. We delete those retained records when the relevant obligation or dispute-related requirement ends; we do not retain them permanently by default. Your case result identifies each retained category, the reason, and any applicable fixed retention period or end condition.

Relevant retained categories may include charging receipts and ledger entries, wallet settlement, refunds and disputes, memberships, monetary rewards and payment references. Each category requires its own specific basis; we do not apply a blanket retention period to all categories.

Uploaded diagnostic reports are kept indefinitely by default. A verified account deletion request is an approved exception to that default, and we remove identifiable diagnostic content for the account.

Our manual review includes backup copies: we identify remaining copies, restrict their use and apply the deletion again before any restored data becomes active. The receipt identifies outstanding backup handling; we do not report all copies erased before that is verified.

Closure is not deletion

Closing your account only disables access.

Closure disables sign-in and revokes credentials once charging, payments, wallet balances and membership requirements are settled. It does not erase your profile, vehicles, transactions or other associated records. To erase data, send the deletion request described above.

More information

See the V2Charge privacy notice for how we process data, the service providers we use, and retention details. This page and that notice cover V2Charge only. They do not replace the policies for other V2Power products.

Contact
